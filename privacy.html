Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 July 2026

1. Overview

Future Proofing Lifestyle Network ("FPL", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect on futureproofinglifestyle.com, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details — name, email address and phone number you provide when joining as a volunteer, logging in, or purchasing a product.
  • Purchase details — products bought, amount, date and payment status. Card, UPI and banking details are handled entirely by our payment partners (such as Razorpay) and never touch our servers.
  • Usage information — basic technical data such as pages visited and device type, used to keep the site working and improve it.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your volunteer or member account.
  • To deliver the products, courses and community access you request.
  • To send you service messages and, if you opt in, updates about FPL books, courses and events. You can unsubscribe anytime.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

4. What we never do

We never sell or rent your personal information to anyone. We share it only with the service providers needed to run FPL — payment processing, email delivery and website hosting — and only to the extent required.

5. Cookies

We use only essential cookies and similar storage needed for login sessions and site functionality. We do not run third-party advertising trackers.

6. Data retention and your rights

We keep your data only as long as your account is active or as required by law. You may request a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account at any time by writing to omjai.vt@gmail.com.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with reputable providers. Payments are processed by PCI-DSS-compliant payment gateways.

8. Children

Our services are intended for users aged 18 and above, or minors using them with a parent or guardian's supervision.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the latest version will always be on this page. Questions: see our Contact page.
